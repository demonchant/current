A clearer path from saving to progress

A goal feels different
when you can follow it.

For people in Nigeria who choose to save some value in digital dollars, the next step can feel scattered: find the rate, check the wallet, understand gas, and later work out whether the position changed.

Current brings those steps together. Compare the live Aave rate for USDC or USDT, see your own Polygon position, set a goal that syncs across devices, and verify each transaction. Rates vary, and stablecoins are not risk-free.

Before you begin

A plan can grow.
It can also change.

Aave supply rates move with the market. No return is promised. Supplying USDC or USDT carries protocol, liquidity, stablecoin, and network risks. Stablecoins do not guarantee protection from naira inflation or exchange-rate changes.

Current uses Polygon mainnet. You need USDC or USDT to supply. Standard transactions need POL for network fees; the optional NIM relay has its own disclosed fee. Check the amount, destination, network, and fee before signing.
