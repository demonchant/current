A clearer route to earning

One flow. Real protocol. Your keys.

Moving from a wallet to a lending market can mean switching tools and interpreting several steps. Current brings the market view, wallet context, and transaction review together so each action is easier to understand.

01 / CHECK

See the market

Read the current Aave USDT supply rate and reserve status from live market data.

02 / REVIEW

Connect your wallet

Use Nimiq Pay to inspect your Polygon balances and review the amount and transaction before signing.

03 / VERIFY

Confirm the outcome

After supply or withdrawal, check the Polygon receipt and position read-back—not just a success message.

Know before you use it. This flow targets Polygon mainnet. You need USDT and POL for gas. Aave rates vary; supply involves protocol, liquidity, stablecoin, and network risks. Current does not guarantee returns or custody your assets.

Why Earn

A Mini App built around yield.

The competition’s Earn category is about putting assets to work. Current uses Nimiq Pay’s wallet access to let a user supply USDT to Aave V3 on Polygon and manage that position from the Mini App.
